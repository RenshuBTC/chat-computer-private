Chat Computer

A computer you talk to: one conversation as the whole computer, on an AI-native operating system that is free and open source, with Meta's Muse Spark as its brain. Still a work in progress, and our entry to the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon.

The video shows Chat Computer's full screen, with Muse Spark answering: your messages from every chat app on the left, every action on the right (each can be reverted), and sites opened by typing them in the chat box. Underneath is Chat Computer's own operating system, written in Rust, whose kernel talks to Meta's Model API over its own network stack and TLS 1.3.
