Chat Computer for Chrome

Chrome adds extensions by itself only from its Web Store, and Chat Computer isn't there yet, so Chrome asks you to add it, in three steps.

  1. Open a new tab, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter (Chrome doesn't let a page or another app open it).
  2. Turn on Developer mode, at the top right.
  3. Choose Load unpacked, and choose the folder you unzipped, called Chat Computer. In the window that opens, click the address bar at its top, paste the path (Ctrl+V), press Enter, then choose Select Folder. In the window that opens, press Command-Shift-G, paste the path (Command-V), press Return, then choose Select.
  4. Chat Computer is then under the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar: pin it, and click it to open Chat Computer in the side panel.
