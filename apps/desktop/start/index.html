Chat Computer for Chrome

Chrome adds extensions by itself only from its Web Store, and Chat Computer isn't there yet, so Chrome asks you to add it, in three steps. Chrome is open, at a page with these steps.

Chrome isn't on this computer: get it at google.com/chrome, then press Open Chrome here. The extension's folder stays where it is.

  1. In Chrome's address bar, type chrome://extensions and press Enter (Chrome doesn't let another app open that page).
  2. Turn on Developer mode, at the top right.
  3. Choose Load unpacked, and choose this folder: In the window that opens, click the address bar at its top, paste the path (Ctrl+V), press Enter, then choose Select Folder. In the window that opens, press Command-Shift-G, paste the path (Command-V), press Return, then choose Select. Its path is copied.
  4. Chat Computer is then under the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar: pin it, and click it to open Chat Computer in the side panel.

Chat Computer as your browser

Sites open in tabs above the conversation. Type a web address in the box for a new tab, or ask Chat Computer anything; + beside the tabs takes you back to the conversation.

It's your default browser: the links you click in other apps open here.

So that the links you click in other apps open here, make it your default browser. Windows asks you to choose that yourself: Settings opens at Chat Computer, where you choose Set default (or, under Web browser, Chat Computer). macOS asks you to confirm it.

Chat Computer's own system

Chat Computer Setup has started.

Chat Computer Setup runs in a window of its own. Windows asks whether it may make changes: choose Yes. Setup then checks this PC and lists everything it's about to change; nothing on your disks changes until you answer y. It needs about 70 GB of free space and the internet. Windows stays, one restart away.

Chat Computer Setup runs in Terminal: answer its questions there. Nothing on your Mac changes until you answer y. It needs about 80 GB of free space and Wi-Fi. macOS, your apps and your files stay, one restart away.
